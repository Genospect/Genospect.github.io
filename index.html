03 / TRACEABLE RESULTS

Evidence, Not Just Outputs

Every scientific conclusion should be traceable.

Genospect connects exact inputs, actors, model or tool versions, parameters, authorization, execution records, outputs, and validation state. M3 returns a bounded result envelope; richer evidence and claim semantics arrive in M4.

OPERATIONAL EXPLAINABILITYShow what ran, on which data, under which policy, and what evidence resulted—without claiming to interpret a model's internal reasoning.
